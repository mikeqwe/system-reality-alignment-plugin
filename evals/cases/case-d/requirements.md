The service requests account creation from an external vendor. HTTP 202 returns an operation ID and means accepted. A signed callback later reports success or failure for that operation. Callbacks can be repeated and delayed; no ordering or idempotent-create contract has been provided. The vendor also exposes a read-only operation-status query. Users must not receive active entitlements until creation succeeds. Support can investigate unresolved operations, but no specific responder or time threshold has been chosen. Keep the design deployable by a small team with one relational database. Do not propose a new messaging platform just for this workflow.
